01. About

I lead quality engineering and automation for large-scale distributed systems — and increasingly, I build the GenAI tooling that quality engineering runs on.

For the past five years at Comcast Technology Solutions, I've led a 12-person QA team while personally architecting the automation frameworks, CI/CD pipelines, and monitoring dashboards they run on daily. Somewhere along the way, the work stopped being just about writing tests and became about designing systems: RAG pipelines that retrieve the right test knowledge, agents that triage failures before a human sees them, and dashboards that turn pipeline noise into a signal engineers actually trust.

I've shipped two production GenAI tools end-to-end — from system design through FastAPI backends, RAG implementation, and deployment — and I still spend most days in Python, Java, Selenium, and Jenkins keeping the fundamentals solid. Good AI-assisted tooling doesn't replace test discipline; it's built on top of it.

GenAI practitioner

Shipped two production GenAI tools at Comcast — ADI Validator and KeystoneIQ — on a GPT-4.1 + RAG architecture, owning system design, the FastAPI backend, the RAG pipeline, and deployment end to end. Completed Anthropic's Claude Code and AI Fluency courses.

End-to-end builder

Designs system architecture, builds REST APIs, implements RAG pipelines, introduces OpenSpec development practices, and integrates self-hosted CI/CD — not just automation scripts sitting on top of someone else's system.
